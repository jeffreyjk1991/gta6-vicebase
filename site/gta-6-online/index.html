GTA 6 Online: release date, multiplayer and crossplay

As of November 20, 2026, GTA VI is a single-player game. Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 Online release date or confirmed PS5/Xbox crossplay.

CHECKED 20 NOV 2026 · OFFICIAL SOURCES

GTA VI launched. GTA VI Online has not.

Rockstar's November 19, 2026 launch announcement confirms GTA VI for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. PlayStation's GTA VI FAQ says it is a single-player experience. No official multiplayer launch date, crossplay support, or cross-save feature has been announced.

Frequently asked questions

When is GTA 6 Online coming out?

Rockstar has not announced an official GTA VI Online release date as of November 20, 2026. Dates circulated by creators are predictions unless supported by Rockstar.

Can I play GTA 6 Online now?

No announced online multiplayer mode is available as part of the November 19 GTA VI console launch. Rockstar and PlayStation describe this launch as a single-player experience.

Does GTA 6 have multiplayer?

The launch version is single-player. Rockstar has not provided a date or feature list for a future GTA VI multiplayer mode.

Will GTA 6 have crossplay between PS5 and Xbox?

There is no official confirmation of GTA VI crossplay. Do not plan to join PS5 and Xbox friends together until an announcement.

Can I transfer my GTA Online character or money to GTA 6?

Rockstar has not announced any transfer of existing GTA Online accounts, characters, money or properties into GTA VI.

Is GTA Online the same as GTA 6 Online?

No. The existing GTA Online refers to GTA V's multiplayer world. GTA VI's November 19 release is a separate single-player game.

Will GTA Online shut down when GTA 6 launches?

Rockstar was still publishing GTA Online updates in November 2026. No GTA Online shutdown announcement is established by the official sources referenced here.

Is GTA 6 co-op or does it support split-screen?

Rockstar's documented GTA VI launch is single-player; no GTA VI co-op or split-screen mode has been officially confirmed.
