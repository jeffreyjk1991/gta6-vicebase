How to open GTA 6 settings on PS5 and Xbox

  1. Pause Grand Theft Auto VI (Options on PS5 or Menu on Xbox).
  2. Select Settings from the Pause Menu.
  3. Use Controller for look/aim sensitivity, Gameplay for targeting and movement, Display for motion blur and HDR, Camera for shake and bob, and UI for subtitles and minimap.
  4. Change one or two values at a time. Try the same short drive or fight to see if it actually feels better.
  5. If something feels worse, use Rockstar's Restore Default or Restore All at the bottom of Settings.

GTA 6 performance: can you unlock 60 FPS?

Rockstar's November 10, 2026 announcement describes GTA VI as targeting 30 FPS on the current console generation. It highlights PSSR image clarity and ray-traced shadows on PS5 Pro, not an official 60 FPS setting. Motion blur, camera and controller options affect how play feels; they do not unlock extra frames. See our GTA 6 FPS guide for the source-backed details.

For motion sickness, start with this comfort preset

Set Motion Blur Strength to 0, turn Camera Shake off, lower Camera Bob, consider turning Weapon Sway off and enable Persistent Center Dot under UI → Reticle. For accessibility, adjust UI Text Size and color settings to your needs. These changes are optional; comfort varies from player to player.

Step-by-step: tune your settings without guesswork

01

Open Pause Menu → Settings. Rockstar confirms the Controller, Gameplay, Display, UI, Camera and Audio areas.

02

Start with Display → Motion Blur Strength 0 and Camera → Comfort → Camera Shake Off if you value clarity.

03

Under Gameplay → Combat, try Targeting Partial and Aim Assist Strength 4. Choose Lock-On or Free Aim if your preference differs.

04

Test a camera sensitivity value of 7 before raising it in small increments; higher is not always better.

05

Use a moving vehicle and a short shootout to test the settings. Tune vehicle camera height and minimap visibility for your play style.

06

Enable subtitles, adjust UI text and use HDR calibration if those help on your setup.

07

If your changes feel worse, use Restore Default or Restore All in Settings; this tool cannot alter your console configuration.

GTA 6 settings FAQ

Where are the GTA 6 settings?

Pause the game and choose Settings. Rockstar confirms tabs for Controller, Gameplay, Display, UI, Camera, Audio, Control Scheme and Account.

What are the best GTA 6 controller settings?

Start with look and aim sensitivity around 7 and adjust in small steps. Use Partial targeting and Aim Assist Strength 4 for an approachable baseline; your ideal values depend on your reactions.

Does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS on PS5 Pro?

Rockstar describes a 30 FPS console target and PS5 Pro PSSR and shadow enhancements, not a guaranteed 60 FPS performance setting.

How do I turn off motion blur in GTA 6?

Pause → Settings → Display → Motion Blur Strength. Try 0 for a clearer view while turning.
