How to open GTA 6 settings on PS5 and Xbox

  1. Pause Grand Theft Auto VI (Options on PS5 or Menu on Xbox).
  2. Select Settings from the Pause Menu.
  3. Use Controller for look/aim sensitivity, Gameplay for targeting and movement, Display for motion blur and HDR, Camera for shake and bob, and UI for subtitles and minimap.
  4. Change one or two values at a time. Try the same short drive or fight to see if it actually feels better.
  5. If something feels worse, use Rockstar's Restore Default or Restore All at the bottom of Settings.

GTA 6 performance: can you unlock 60 FPS?

Rockstar's November 10, 2026 announcement describes GTA VI as targeting 30 FPS on the current console generation. It highlights PSSR image clarity and ray-traced shadows on PS5 Pro, not an official 60 FPS setting. Motion blur, camera and controller options affect how play feels; they do not unlock extra frames. See our GTA 6 FPS guide for the source-backed details.

For motion sickness, start with this comfort preset

Set Motion Blur Strength to 0, turn Camera Shake off, lower Camera Bob, consider turning Weapon Sway off and enable Persistent Center Dot under UI → Reticle. For accessibility, adjust UI Text Size and color settings to your needs. These changes are optional; comfort varies from player to player.

Step-by-step guide

01

Open Pause Menu → Settings and review Controller, Gameplay, Display, UI, Camera and Audio. The categories are listed by Rockstar Support.

02

Under Display, try Motion Blur Strength 0. In Camera → Comfort, turn Camera Shake off and reduce Camera Bob if motion bothers you.

03

Under Gameplay → Combat, begin with Partial targeting and Aim Assist Strength 4; use Lock-On for more help or Free Aim for a challenge.

04

In Controller, start with Look and Aim Sensitivity around 7. Test 9 or 10 for look speed only if you feel sluggish. Adjust one step at a time.

05

Under Gameplay → Movement, test Run for faster on-foot play; for driving, compare a medium or higher vehicle-camera height.

06

Use UI → Navigation to keep the Minimap visible and UI → Subtitles & Captions to enable readable dialogue if helpful.

07

Calibrate HDR for your TV, use TV Game Mode, and try the interactive presets on this page. These do not unlock a 60 FPS mode.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best GTA 6 settings on PS5?

Start with Motion Blur Strength 0, Camera Shake Off, Targeting Partial, Aim Assist Strength 4 and look/aim sensitivity comfortable to you. Use the settings finder for driving, combat and cinematic profiles.

Does GTA 6 have a 60 FPS mode?

Rockstar targets 30 FPS on consoles in its official November 10 update. It did not promise a 60 FPS performance mode. PS5 Pro adds image clarity and ray-traced shadows.

Where is aim assist in GTA 6?

Open Pause Menu → Settings → Gameplay → Combat, then change Targeting and Aim Assist Strength.

How can I reduce GTA 6 motion sickness?

Try Motion Blur Strength 0, Camera Shake Off, Camera Bob 0–2 and a Persistent Center Dot, then adjust to comfort.

Are the settings presets official?

No. The menu categories are documented by Rockstar; GTASIX suggested values are independent, subjective starting points.

Are GTA 6 PC graphics settings available?

Rockstar has announced the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, not an official PC version or PC graphics options.
