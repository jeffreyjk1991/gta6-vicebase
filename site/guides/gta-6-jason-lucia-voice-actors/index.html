GTA 6 Voice Actors: Jason Duval, Lucia and Brian

Meet the actors behind GTA VI's protagonists and see which roles were publicly confirmed at launch.

Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval, and Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos. Both acknowledged their roles in a joint public November 19, 2026 statement reported by Video Games Chronicle. Stephen Root has also confirmed involvement as Brian Heder.
