PC rumors versus Rockstar's announced platforms

Rockstar's official November 2026 GTA VI release lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That does not confirm a Windows launch date, minimum CPU, graphics card, RAM requirement or storage footprint. Any circulated "GTA 6 minimum PC specs" table should be treated as unofficial unless Rockstar publishes it.

For a new PC purchase, avoid spending money based on guessed specifications. The reliable path is to monitor Rockstar's GTA VI page and the console performance guide for separately documented information.
