PS5 SSD requirements for GTA 6: compatibility checklist

Learn the exact M.2 NVMe specification to check before buying an SSD.

CONFIRMED UPDATED NOV 18, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

PS5 supports PCIe Gen4 x4 M.2 NVMe SSDs (Key M), 250 GB–8 TB, with effective heat dissipation. Sony recommends sequential reads of 5,500 MB/s or faster.

Which SSD specifications actually matter?

Sony's M.2 requirements apply to GTA VI as they do to other PS5 games. A drive must be PCIe Gen4 ×4 NVMe (M Key), between 250 GB and 8 TB, with a compatible form factor. Sony recommends a sequential read speed of 5,500 MB/s or faster. Cooling hardware is required, and total drive-plus-heatsink thickness must fit the console expansion bay.

Don't buy only because a product advertises "for GTA 6": compatibility is about Sony's slot and thermal limits, not game-specific branding. A step-by-step installation guide and Sony's official specifications are available.

Step-by-step guide

01

Choose an M.2 NVMe PCIe Gen4 x4 SSD with Key M; a SATA M.2 drive is not compatible.

02

Verify a supported length/form factor: 2230, 2242, 2260, 2280 or 22110.

03

Check it meets Sony’s physical clearance limits, especially if using an attached heatsink.

04

Use a suitable heatsink/cooling structure and update PS5 system software.

05

Install according to Sony’s guide, format the drive on PS5, then download/move GTA 6 to it.

Frequently asked questions

Can GTA 6 run from a PS5 M.2 SSD?

Yes. Sony supports playing PS5 games directly from compatible M.2 expansion storage.

Is 5,500 MB/s mandatory?

Sony recommends 5,500 MB/s or faster; check all listed requirements.

Can I install a SATA SSD?

No. Sony does not support M.2 SATA SSDs in the PS5 expansion slot.
