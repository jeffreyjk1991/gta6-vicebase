How Long Is GTA 6? Story Hours, Chapters and 100% Completion

Compare early GTA VI story completion estimates with a tracked sample and understand why exploration and 100% take longer.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

GTA VI has five story chapters. PowerPyx's launch walkthrough estimates roughly 60–80 hours for the story with ordinary play. A separate voluntary tracking sample reported a median near 55 hours for 275 finishers on November 24, but that small, self-selected early cohort is not representative of all players. Completionist time varies dramatically with collectibles and side activities.

Step-by-step guide

01

Decide what you mean by finishing: main story credits, story plus side quests, or full 100% completion. These are not interchangeable.

02

For a typical story estimate, use PowerPyx's reported 60–80-hour range for the five-chapter campaign, treating it as an editorial estimate rather than a guarantee.

03

Compare that with self-reported community trackers only if you accept sample bias; early finishers tend to play faster and for longer sessions.

04

Track your own time by chapter rather than assuming every chapter is equal in length. Some contain more story objectives and more optional diversions.

05

If you only have a few hours a week, divide your remaining main missions by your personal pace instead of using a universal countdown.

06

For 100%, plan time for collectibles, activity challenges and exploration beyond the story; the figure will depend on what you skip, look up or do naturally.

07

Keep manual saves around chapter boundaries if you care about revisiting earlier story events; PowerPyx reports mission replay is not supported.

Frequently asked questions

How many chapters are in GTA 6?

PowerPyx lists five story chapters in its full walkthrough.

How many hours to finish GTA 6 story?

PowerPyx estimates around 60–80 hours; self-selected tracking samples of early finishers may run faster.

Can you finish GTA 6 in 20 hours?

Some speed-focused players may do so, but it is well below the reported typical story estimate and would omit much exploration.

How long does GTA 6 100% completion take?

There is no single credible universal time. Challenges, collectibles and side missions add substantial variation.

Why do playtime websites give different numbers?

They may count different goals and populations; early samples can overrepresent highly motivated or fast players.
