Frequently asked questions

Rockstar states PlayStation digital codes can be region-restricted; match the account region.

Do Xbox codes have regional restrictions?

Rockstar’s FAQ describes Xbox codes as not region-locked.

Does preload require a separate purchase?

It requires an eligible existing preorder or redeemed copy.
