How to Switch Between Jason and Lucia in GTA 6

Find the Partner Menu buttons on PlayStation and Xbox and understand the Chapter 2 story restrictions.

HANDS-ON REPORTSUPDATED NOV 19, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

After the story unlocks free switching, hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox Series X|S. Switching is restricted during some missions and early story events.

EDITORIAL SOURCE NOTE. This is a dated summary of launch-day reports, not a claim of GTASIX first-hand testing. Options and scores may change.

What to know

01

Play through the opening; hands-on guides place free character switching in Chapter 2.

02

During free roam, hold Right on the D-pad to access the Partner Menu.

03

Press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to switch between Jason and Lucia.

04

If a character is greyed out, complete your current mission or advance the story.

05

The Partner Menu can also offer an Invite option; that is different from switching.

06

Check your Control Scheme if you changed your controller mapping.

Frequently asked questions

How do I switch to Lucia on PS5?

Hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 once free switching is unlocked.

How do I switch on Xbox?

Hold Right on the D-pad and press RB.

When does character switching unlock?

After progression into Chapter 2, according to multiple launch-day guides.

Why can I not switch?

Some missions and story sequences temporarily lock the protagonist.
