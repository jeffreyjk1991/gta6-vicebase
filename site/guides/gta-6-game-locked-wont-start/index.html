GTA 6 Won't Start or Is Locked: PS5 & Xbox License Errors

Troubleshoot GTA 6 padlock icons, launch errors and code redemption issues with official PlayStation and Xbox steps.

SOURCE-BACKED CHECKLISTUPDATED NOV 20, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

For a locked GTA VI purchase, confirm the purchasing or redemption account, download status and store region. On PS5, Sony recommends checking Console Sharing and Offline Play and using Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licenses. On Xbox, verify Owned Games and search the exact Microsoft error code.

Step-by-step troubleshooting

01

Confirm that GTA VI appears in the purchase history or owned-games library of the account currently signed in.

02

If you redeemed a code, ensure it matches your PlayStation account region; Rockstar warns that some PlayStation codes are region-specific. Xbox code policies differ.

03

Verify that the game is fully installed and that no console or GTA VI update is pending.

04

PS5: for padlocks or inaccessible purchases, Sony says to check Console Sharing and Offline Play, then restore licenses under Settings → Users and Accounts → Other.

05

Xbox: inspect My games & apps and the exact error message, then consult Microsoft's error-code guide. Do not assume all launch codes share a cause.

06

If you see a code such as CE-107891-6, use the official PlayStation error lookup. Player reports are not sufficient to diagnose the error.

07

Only consider canceling a download or reinstalling after confirming ownership and following the manufacturer's official sequence. Do not delete save data.

Frequently asked questions

Why is GTA 6 locked on PS5?

It may involve account ownership, console sharing, region or license problems. Check purchase history and Sony's Restore Licenses guidance.

How do I restore GTA 6 licenses on PS5?

Go to Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licenses. Sony states this does not affect your saved data.

What does GTA 6 error CE-107891-6 mean?

Players report seeing this error, but GTASIX does not assign a cause without Sony's official description. Look it up on PlayStation Support.

Why can't I redeem my GTA 6 code?

Rockstar documents PlayStation region restrictions on some download codes. Check the code's packaging or seller region against your PlayStation account region.

Can I play GTA 6 on PS4?

No PS4 launch version was announced. GTA VI was released for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
