GTA 6 Autosave Not Working or Progress Lost: Protect Your Saves

Understand the GTA 6 autosave bug and first patch, protect manual saves and avoid overwriting your remaining progress.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

Rockstar's November 23 patch addressed a GTA VI autosave failure. That does not guarantee already-lost progress can be restored. Before retrying, identify the newest manual and autosave entries without replacing them. In free roam, use the pause menu's Game → Save Game and a separate slot before ending a session.

Step-by-step guide

01

Check your installed GTA VI version and install the available official update; Rockstar's November 23 patch specifically includes an autosave fix.

02

Open Pause Menu → Game → Load Game to inspect the timestamps of available manual saves and autosave entries. Do not select a slot to overwrite while investigating.

03

If your current playthrough is accessible, leave active missions and police pursuit, then use Game → Save Game in free roam to create a new manual slot.

04

Do not rely on quick return to the console home screen. Confirm your manual save's timestamp before quitting normally.

05

PS5: examine local and cloud saved-data versions without immediately choosing an upload/download direction. Xbox: review cloud sync prompts before accepting a conflicting copy.

06

If you have already loaded an older save, do not assume the patch can recreate later unsaved events. Record the timeline and contact Rockstar support if saves are missing.

07

Before a major heist, story branch or long collectible session, keep a separate save so an accidental overwrite does not erase your only rollback point.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 autosave automatically?

Yes, but reports show one rolling autosave, not a history of every activity. Manual saves offer additional rollback points.

How many manual save slots does GTA 6 have?

GamesRadar's post-launch guide reports 15 manual slots plus a separate autosave.

Why is Save Game grayed out?

It is not generally available during a mission or active danger; finish or leave that activity and retry in free roam.

Will the November 23 update restore lost progress?

Rockstar confirms a fix to future autosaving behavior but does not state it restores previously overwritten saves.

Should I delete my cloud saves to fix the problem?

No. Inspect timestamps and preserve both copies until you know which contains the latest progress.
