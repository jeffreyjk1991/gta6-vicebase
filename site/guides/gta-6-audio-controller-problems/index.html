GTA 6 Audio Cutting Out or Controller Not Working: PS5/Xbox

Find safe checks for interrupted audio, headset sound problems, DualSense or Xbox controller input and unusual vibration while playing GTA VI.

PLATFORM GUIDANCE UPDATED NOV 20, 20265 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

If GTA VI audio cuts out or controller vibration feels wrong, test whether the problem happens in other games, check the correct output device and controller settings, restart GTA VI, and install available console/controller updates. Launch-day players reported these symptoms, but the underlying cause is not confirmed.

Step-by-step guide

01

Check whether the problem happens only in GTA VI or also in the console's menus and other games; this helps separate game-specific symptoms from hardware issues.

02

For audio, test TV speakers and headset separately, and verify which output device the console selected.

03

PS5: review Settings → Sound and the Output Device, and update console and DualSense software if prompted.

04

Xbox: check Settings → General → Volume & audio output, headset connection and available controller firmware updates.

05

If vibration is unusual, test lowering vibration intensity or adaptive-trigger resistance in GTA VI or console controller settings.

06

Close and restart the game once; note the exact mission, time and game version if the problem recurs.

07

Do not install unknown audio drivers or controller-fix apps from unofficial GTA VI sites. If other games are affected, use manufacturer support.

Frequently asked questions

Why is GTA 6 sound cutting out?

Headset and in-game audio interruptions have been reported, but no single confirmed cause or universal fix is established.

Why does my DualSense vibrate continuously in GTA 6?

Some launch-day players reported unusual vibration; test the controller outside GTA VI and reduce vibration as a temporary comfort check.

How do I change the audio output on PS5?

Open PS5 Settings → Sound and check the selected Output Device.

What if audio cuts out only with headphones?

Test TV speakers, reconnect the headset and verify output-device selection and firmware updates.

Do I need to download a GTA 6 controller driver?

No unofficial download is recommended; use console manufacturers' firmware and GTA VI's built-in control settings.
