Step-by-step guide

01

Complete the main story; launch-day guides say cheats remain locked until then.

02

Open Pause Menu → Progress → Cheats to see which cheats you have unlocked.

03

In free roam, open your phone with D-pad Up, then hold L1+R1 on PS5 (LB+RB on Xbox), and enter a code using D-pad directions.

04

The reported $250,000 cheat is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right, after story completion.

05

Save your progress first. Cheats reportedly disable saving, trophies and achievements for the active session.

06

Do not trust sites asking for account passwords, unofficial game installers or cheat-generator downloads.
