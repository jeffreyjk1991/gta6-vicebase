GTA 6 Partner Won't Hand Over Bags to a Fence? Fixes

Check the first patch if a partner will not hand over a loot bag for sale to a fence, and protect goods while troubleshooting.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

Rockstar's November 23 update addressed a problem where a partner did not hand over bags so they could be sold to fences. Install the latest GTA VI update, confirm you are at the intended fence with the correct goods, and retry a safe transaction; do not discard loot or overwrite your only save.

Fence transaction checklist

CheckpointWhat to verify
UpdateThe November 23 patch is installed
Correct transactionYou are at a fence and the expected handover prompt is active
Progress protectedExisting goods and latest save have not been discarded
RepeatabilityNote which partner, which fence, console version and exact error

Unlike a general money-making guide, this page covers a named bug in Rockstar's official November 23 notes. If it continues, use the console Help Center or the latest patch checker.

VERIFICATION NOTE. Official and third-party reports are sourced, but GTASIX has not independently tested all details. Game updates may change the steps.

Step-by-step guide

01

Confirm whether you are at a fence and the transaction specifically asks your partner to hand over a bag; do not conflate this with an ATM deposit or ordinary car sale.

02

Check the GTA VI version and install the November 23 update, which explicitly fixes a partner bag handover failure.

03

Exit the transaction menu without leaving or discarding goods. Re-enter once after the update and a normal game restart.

04

Check whether the in-game map or task gives you a specific delivery contact, objective or shop; not every type of loot is handled identically.

05

Before repeating a risky transaction, create a manual save where the game allows it. Do not overwrite your only safe checkpoint.

06

If the partner still refuses to hand over a bag, note the mission or seller, console, game version, and the exact on-screen prompt for Rockstar Support.

07

Avoid third-party save editors, account logins or exploit downloads marketed as a 'fence fix'.

Frequently asked questions

Why won't my GTA 6 partner hand over stolen goods?

Rockstar listed a specific partner handover bug in the November 23 GTA VI patch notes.

Did the latest GTA 6 update fix the fence bug?

The official notes say a failure involving handing over bags for sale to fences was addressed; this does not rule out every related problem.

Is this the same as the GTA 6 money glitch?

No. A failed intended handover at a fence is a game bug; money-loop exploits are a different subject.

Should I throw away the bag and try again?

No. Protect existing progress and test a safe retry after updating before discarding inventory.

Where can I report the problem?

Use Rockstar Games Customer Support with your console, installed version, mission and the precise failed transaction.
