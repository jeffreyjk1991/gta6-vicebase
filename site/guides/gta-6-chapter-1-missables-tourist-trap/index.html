GTA 6 Chapter 1 Missables: What to Do Before Tourist Trap

Before starting Tourist Trap, finish Shirley's two optional quests and keep a Chapter 1 save if you want to preserve the experience.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

PowerPyx's walkthrough confirms that Chapter 1 optional jobs Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter disappear when the mission Tourist Trap begins. Neither is required for GTA VI trophies or 100% completion. If you want to play them, complete both in Key Lento as Jason and create a separate manual save before progressing.

Step-by-step guide

01

Before accepting Tourist Trap (Take Up Mattia's Offer), consider creating a separate manual save during free roam.

02

After completing the Chapter 1 prerequisites and unlocking Pipework, find Shirley near Jason's neighborhood in Key Lento and start Get Shirley's Rum.

03

Follow Shirley's objective to ask the liquor store clerk for her rum, then return it. This unlocks the follow-up quest.

04

Complete Steal the Scooter for Shirley before moving the chapter forward. PowerPyx identifies it as another Chapter 1-exclusive optional quest.

05

Check your available time-limited side markers. PowerPyx says an hourglass indicates a mission can expire; do those you care about as they appear.

06

Start Tourist Trap only once you are comfortable leaving these Chapter 1-exclusive jobs behind.

07

If you already passed this point, do not panic about Platinum or 100%. According to PowerPyx, these optional missables are not required for those goals.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Chapter 1 point of no return in GTA 6?

The Tourist Trap mission is reported as the point after which Shirley's Chapter 1 optional jobs cannot be completed.

Is Get Shirley's Rum missable?

Yes. PowerPyx says it becomes unavailable when Tourist Trap starts.

Can you do Steal the Scooter after Chapter 1?

No. The walkthrough describes this optional job as Chapter 1-exclusive.

Will missing Shirley's quests prevent 100% completion?

No. PowerPyx says these two optional quests do not affect trophies or required completion.

Can you replay story missions in GTA 6?

PowerPyx reports no standard mission replay feature, so preserve manual chapter saves if you wish to revisit earlier stages.
