GTA 6 Best Aim Assist Settings: Lock-On, Partial or Free Aim?

Set up easier shooting or manual aim in GTA 6 with clear controller menu paths and three practical, adjustable presets.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

On PS5 and Xbox, open Pause Menu → Settings → Gameplay → Combat to find Targeting. Lock-On attempts to snap to the nearest target; Partial helps without locking; Free Aim provides manual control. Begin with Lock-On for simpler combat or Partial for balance, then tune aim sensitivity and assist while testing one repeatable encounter.

Step-by-step guide

01

Pause GTA VI and open Settings, then Gameplay → Combat to locate Targeting. Labels and the number of available options may change with updates.

02

Choose Lock-On if you want aiming to select nearby enemies. Choose Partial for assisted control without a hard snap, or Free Aim for a deliberate challenge.

03

For easier combat, begin with Lock-On and a medium aim-assist value. Leave Shooting Assist and Quick Draw on while learning the controls.

04

For balanced play, try Partial, moderate Aim Assist Strength, and a comfortable right-stick sensitivity. Test before changing multiple settings at once.

05

For manual aim, select Free Aim and reduce Aim Assist Strength; turn off Shooting Assist and Drive-By Assist if you want truly minimal assistance.

06

Open Controller → Aim & Look Sensitivity to adjust horizontal and vertical aim speed in small increments. Check UI → Reticle for hit markers and center-dot visibility.

07

Test in an ordinary repeatable encounter, not a difficult mission checkpoint. Save or record your previous values so you can revert.

Frequently asked questions

Where is aim assist in GTA 6?

The Targeting and Aim Assist Strength options are reported under Pause Menu → Settings → Gameplay → Combat.

What is the easiest GTA 6 Targeting mode?

Lock-On offers the greatest assistance by attempting to snap to a nearby target.

Is Partial better than Lock-On?

Partial gives a less automated feeling. Which is better depends on the player's comfort and desired challenge.

What settings make GTA 6 aiming fully manual?

Free Aim with Shooting Assist and Drive-By Assist disabled and assist strength lowered offers the most direct manual control.

How do I change the crosshair?

Look in Settings → UI → Reticle, including hit markers and visibility options.
