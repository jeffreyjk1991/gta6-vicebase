GTA 6 release date, launch time & countdown

Grand Theft Auto VI releases November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar says the game unlocks at midnight local time on those consoles. PC has not been announced for launch.

Step-by-step guide

01

Confirm your platform is PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X or Xbox Series S; previous-generation consoles are not listed for launch.

02

Open the game on your console and inspect its own release/unlock countdown; your storefront is the last word for your account and territory.

03

Install the preloaded files before November 19; downloading before release does not mean early gameplay access.

04

Turn on automatic updates and leave extra free storage for any launch-day patch.
