GTA 6 Fast Travel: Taxi, Bus, Train and RydeMe Explained

Learn how GTA 6 transportation works, when taxis and RydeMe become available and how to skip rides on PS5 or Xbox.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

GTA VI offers fast-travel options through taxis, public transit and RydeMe rather than an unrestricted teleport-from-anywhere map. GamesRadar reports Paradise Taxi access during Chapter 1 and the RydeMe phone app in Chapter 2. For a taxi ride, request a destination, then use the on-screen skip option once onboard; an additional fee may apply.

Step-by-step guide

01

Decide whether you need a taxi on demand, a scheduled public bus/train, or the RydeMe rideshare app.

02

To use Paradise Taxi, unlock its contact during Chapter 1, open the in-game phone with D-pad Up, then call from Contacts.

03

Choose a destination and get in the taxi. GamesRadar reports you can focus on the driver using L2/LT and select a skip-ride action using Triangle/Y for an extra charge.

04

For buses or trains, find a real transit stop or station and wait for service, then board and choose a destination along the route.

05

Once aboard transit, follow the in-game option to skip the remainder of the trip if available.

06

During Chapter 2, RydeMe is reported to become accessible in the phone. It requests a ride and draws the fare from the in-game online bank balance.

07

Check the fare and your cash before requesting an expensive journey; fast travel convenience is not free and different services can charge different accounts.

Frequently asked questions

Can you fast travel in GTA 6?

Yes, through transport options such as taxis, buses, trains and RydeMe, according to GamesRadar's launch guide.

How do you call a taxi in GTA 6?

Unlock the Paradise Taxi contact during Chapter 1, then open the phone and call the service.

When do you unlock RydeMe?

GamesRadar reports RydeMe unlocks during Chapter 2.

Can you skip a taxi ride?

Yes. A reported skip-ride option appears by focusing on the driver, with a separate fee.

Is public transit free?

The reviewed guide describes paid transportation services rather than free teleportation; exact fares and coverage depend on the ride.
