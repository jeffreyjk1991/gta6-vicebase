GTA 6 Blurry Graphics, Input Lag or Stuttering: What Helps?

Improve perceived GTA 6 image clarity and understand what motion blur, TV latency, HDR and console 30 FPS targets really mean.

GTA VI's console performance target is 30 FPS according to Rockstar's pre-launch technical announcement. Motion Blur Strength at 0 may improve clarity while turning, while TV Game Mode and HDR calibration can help latency and image quality. These settings do not unlock 60 FPS.

Step-by-step troubleshooting

01

Decide whether you see blurring when turning quickly, reduced sharpness at long distance, flickering graphics or delayed controller response. These may have different causes.

02

Try Motion Blur Strength 0 in GTA VI's Display menu, especially if fast driving makes the scene hard to read.

03

Check that your TV or monitor is in an appropriate Game Mode to reduce display-side latency.

04

If you use HDR, calibrate brightness with your TV and console. A mismatched HDR output can look washed out or overly dark.

05

Confirm your console's expected video resolution and the game and system software versions; do not assume all Series S, PS5 and PS5 Pro output looks identical.

06

Rockstar's official technical note discusses a 30 FPS target and PS5 Pro image clarity. No official 60 FPS toggle is established by the sources cited here.

07

Persistent visual glitches in the same location should be captured with a screenshot, game version and console model for a support report.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS on PS5?

Rockstar described a 30 FPS target for consoles. No verified official setting unlocks 60 FPS as of November 20.

Why does GTA 6 look blurry on Xbox Series S?

Some players report reduced distant clarity on Series S. Adjusting motion blur can change appearance, but the complaint alone does not diagnose a bug.

Can I turn off motion blur in GTA 6?

Launch-day menu documentation describes Motion Blur Strength under Display. Try 0 and compare.

Will TV Game Mode increase GTA 6 FPS?

It can reduce television processing latency, but it does not make the console render more game frames.

How do I improve GTA 6 HDR?

Calibrate HDR on the console and television; compare scenes with HDR on and off without assuming one is always correct.
