GTA 6 Phone Call Not Triggering? Fix the Missing Next Mission

Find out whether a missing GTA 6 story phone call is a mission requirement, a timing issue or the bug addressed in the first update.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

Rockstar confirmed it fixed phone calls that failed to trigger, including calls that unlock missions, in the November 23 GTA VI update. First install the patch, check the in-game objective or active marker, try a brief period of free roam, and then retry the relevant story checkpoint. Do not assume every delayed call is a bug.

Step-by-step guide

01

Record the exact mission you last completed and whether a phone call, map marker or in-game task tells you what is next.

02

Install the November 23 or newer officially documented GTA VI update; Rockstar's first patch addressed failed story-progression calls.

03

Check the phone's contacts and messages plus the active mission objective. A missing marker and a missing incoming call are different problems.

04

If the last mission told you to wait, spend a short period in free roam on an available activity. Some scheduled calls are intended to arrive later.

05

Try switching between Jason and Lucia only when allowed by the game, and recheck available story markers. Do not assume every character receives the same calls.

06

If still blocked, save manually outside a mission, restart the game and reload the latest safe slot. Avoid deleting saves.

07

Use the spoiler-controlled mission helper with the last mission name; if a specific trigger still fails after updating, report the console, version and steps to Rockstar.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I have no new missions in GTA 6?

Some story steps require a call or prerequisite; the November 23 update also fixed faulty calls that prevented progression.

What patch fixed GTA 6 missing calls?

Rockstar's November 23, 2026 launch update lists phone-call trigger fixes, including story and mission-unlocking calls.

Will switching characters start the next mission?

Only when the next available objective belongs to that character; use the current map and mission instructions instead of treating switching as a universal fix.

How long must I wait for the phone to ring?

There is no single official timer for every mission. Check the specific mission walkthrough and your current objective.

What if there is no mission marker after updating?

Use the mission helper to verify the previous objective, then report a reproducible progression bug if all prerequisites are met.
