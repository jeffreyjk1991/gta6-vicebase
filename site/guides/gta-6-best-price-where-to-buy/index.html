Where to buy GTA 6 cheapest: U.S. PS5 and Xbox deals

Compare $79.99 Standard Edition prices at major U.S. retailers, potential rewards and code-in-box pitfalls.

CONFIRMED UPDATED NOV 18, 20265 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

As checked November 18, 2026, the GTA VI Standard Edition is $79.99 at PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Walmart, Target and Best Buy. Discount eligibility, rewards and checkout taxes determine the actual cheapest final price. The current retail case contains a download code, not a disc.

Compare final cost, not just the sticker price

On November 18, 2026, the U.S. Standard Edition listings reviewed at major stores were $79.99 before tax. Those are snapshots, not a live price feed. Your final cost can be different because of regional tax, pickup or shipping fees, retailer credit, and eligibility for card or loyalty discounts.

The current physical-looking retail packaging is code-in-box; the box contains a digital code, not a game disc. Rockstar also advises PlayStation buyers to match the purchase region to the account's redemption region. For Xbox, Rockstar's published FAQ says codes are not region-locked. Read the full store comparison and check the retailer's checkout conditions before buying.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest official GTA 6 price?

The mainstream U.S. Standard Edition listings checked November 18, 2026 were $79.99 before tax. Store-specific or card discounts may change your final total.

Is the GTA 6 physical edition on disc?

No. Rockstar says the physical code-in-box edition contains a download code, not a disc.

Does Target Circle Card save 5% on GTA 6?

Target generally advertises a 5% discount for eligible purchases with its Circle Card. GTA software is excluded from some separate promotions. Check actual eligibility in your cart.
