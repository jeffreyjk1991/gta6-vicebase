GTA 6 Update 01.002.002: Official Patch Notes and What Changed

See the November 23 GTA VI patch, seven official fixes, version checks and what to do if the same problem continues.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

Rockstar's November 23, 2026 GTA VI launch update addresses crashes, frozen weather, autosave, missing phone calls needed for missions, stuck Wanted Levels, an unusable gym deadlift bar and missing partner bag handovers at fences. PS5 is reported as version 01.002.002 and Xbox as 1.0.2.2. The patch does not promise to fix every crash or recover overwritten progress.

Reported install sizes: roughly 603 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox. These are publisher-reported download figures, not the game's total installed footprint. Rockstar's official support notes remain the authority for what was fixed.

Step-by-step guide

01

Read the November 23 patch list on Rockstar Support before assuming an internet rumor is an official change.

02

Check the installed game version in your console's game-information screen. Published reports identify PS5 as 01.002.002 and Xbox as 1.0.2.2; numbering can differ by platform.

03

PS5: highlight GTA VI on the home screen, press Options, select Check for Update, then follow Sony's download instructions.

04

Xbox Series X|S: open My games & apps, then Manage and Updates. Select the GTA VI update if one is pending.

05

After the update, restart the game normally and retry the exact symptom—mission call, autosave, fence transaction, or Wanted Level—that previously failed.

06

If the bug persists, note the game version and console model and consult Rockstar Support. The notes identify categories of fixes, not every possible cause.

07

Do not overwrite or delete local/cloud save data in order to force an update; patch installation and save deletion are separate actions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the latest GTA 6 patch on November 24, 2026?

The latest Rockstar-documented launch update is dated November 23 and addresses seven categories of gameplay bugs.

How big is GTA 6 update 01.002.002?

Gaming publications report approximately 604 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox; actual downloads may vary.

Does the update fix GTA 6 autosave?

Rockstar lists a fix for a condition where the game did not autosave correctly. It does not promise restoration of already-lost progress.

Did the update add GTA 6 Online or a 60 FPS mode?

Neither feature appears in the published patch notes.

Why is my game still crashing after the patch?

The notes say several crashes were fixed, not all. Update and use the console-specific troubleshooting guide.
