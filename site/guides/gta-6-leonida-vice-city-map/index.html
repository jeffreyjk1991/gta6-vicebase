Rockstar confirms the fictional state of Leonida and Vice City. Some fan maps reconstruct landmarks from trailer imagery, but they are not authoritative representations of the final in-game world.

Official Leonida regions versus community map estimates

Rockstar names Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. Those are real published region names in the fictional world of Leonida. A fan reconstruction may show substantially more detail, such as individual streets or building footprints, but those coordinates have not been verified against the released game.

Our interactive GTA 6 map has two clearly distinguished map modes: a high-resolution community reconstruction and an original stylized overview. You can search community landmark names, zoom to city blocks and save private pins. Treat a mapped storefront as an estimate, not proof that a building is enterable or that a mission begins there.
