Frequently asked questions

Does every trailer shot prove a playable feature?

No. Even in-game footage can contain scripted or cutscene moments.

Where should I watch official GTA 6 videos?

Can I trust a “100% leaked mission list”?

Treat it as unverified unless independently supported by the released game.
