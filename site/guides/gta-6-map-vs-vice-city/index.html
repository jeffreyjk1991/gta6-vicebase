GTA 6 Map vs the Original Vice City: Size, Places and Nostalgia

Explore the difference between the original Vice City and GTA VI's Leonida without confusing familiar scenery with identical streets or unsupported map-size claims.

SOURCE-BASED COMPARISON UPDATED NOV 20, 20265 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

The original GTA: Vice City was released in 2002 and depicts the 1980s. GTA VI returns to Vice City within the wider state of Leonida. Rockstar provides an official Leonida map, but the sources linked here do not establish a directly comparable area measurement for both games. Use them to compare places and geography; a precise size multiplier needs a published measurement method.

Explore beyond the city

Rockstar's Only in Leonida directory introduces Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. Treat these as starting points for exploring the setting, not as a mission order.

Open the official printable Leonida map, browse the GTASIX map explorer, or read our driving and handling guide. If you are looking for the next story objective, use the mission helper.

Step-by-step guide

01

Start with Rockstar's official Leonida map. Keep the game's own map available when choosing a route, since a printable overview does not provide your current objective or progress.

02

Compare like with like: the Vice City urban area and the entire state of Leonida are different subjects. State clearly which boundary a comparison uses.

03

For a size comparison, require a scale, a defined boundary and a consistent decision about land versus water. Matching two images to the same screen width does not establish equal scale.

04

For a nostalgia trip, choose one theme: beachfront architecture, a skyline, nightlife, bridges or a familiar place name. Capture the game version and location beside each screenshot.

05

Separate a shared place name from a verified returning building. Similar colors, signs or silhouettes can make a useful visual comparison without proving that an old interior, mission or exact street layout returns.

06

Use a short local exploration route between places you can identify on your in-game map. If an objective is active, follow that objective first rather than a suggested sightseeing path.

07

Label observations as observations. If you publish an area estimate or a claimed matching location, include the evidence and method so another player can check it.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 set on the original Vice City map?

Rockstar places GTA VI in Vice City and Leonida. A returning city name does not establish that the 2002 game's street plan, interiors or mission locations are reproduced exactly.

How many times bigger is the GTA 6 map than Vice City?

We have not verified a directly comparable measurement for those two maps from the sources linked here. A useful numerical answer must define the area, scale, land and water treatment, and measurement method.

Where can I find the official GTA 6 map?

Rockstar's GTA VI Media page offers the Leonida map and cover-art poster in print-ready formats. Use the official map link in this guide.

Can I use an old Vice City walkthrough for GTA 6?

Use a guide for the game and mission you are playing. A familiar neighborhood or landmark name is not enough to transfer an old mission solution.

How can I compare locations without story spoilers?

Compare public scenery and map labels first. Leave mission objectives, character outcomes and interior reveals out of the comparison until the reader chooses to see them.
