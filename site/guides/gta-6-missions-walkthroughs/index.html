Frequently asked questions

Does this site have every GTA 6 mission walkthrough?

No. The helper provides sourced hints for four early missions and general gameplay checklists. A linked editorial directory provides broader coverage.

Will I see story spoilers?

Specific hints require selecting a mission and revealing details. Codes are hidden behind separate controls.

Can I save a help checklist?

Yes. The mission helper can save your plan on this device when you choose Save plan. It does not synchronize with your game save.

Does everyone play missions in one fixed order?

Do not use a directory number as a universal progression order. Some early missions can be attempted in different orders after the opening.
