GTA 6 Safe Codes: Lab Fees, Brian's Lockup and Keypad Tips

The two commonly confused Lab Fees codes, where they belong, why leading zeros matter and how to check other safes safely.

SOURCE-BASED GUIDEUPDATED NOV 24, 20266 MIN READ

In the Chapter 1 Lab Fees mission, GTA BOOM's hands-on walkthrough gives 0420 for the Moonstone Boutique lab door and 7700 for Brian's lockup. They open different doors. If a code does not work, check that you are at the correct keypad, include any leading zeroes and read the in-game clue for that specific job.

Step-by-step guide

01

In the Chapter 1 Lab Fees mission, identify which door you need: the lab behind Moonstone Boutique or Brian's separate lockup.

02

At Moonstone Boutique's lab keypad enter 0-4-2-0, including the leading zero. The clerk gives this number during the mission.

03

At Brian's lockup use 7-7-0-0, which Brian provides by phone. This is not the boutique lab door.

04

If the input is rejected, clear the keypad and enter four digits slowly. Check whether your character is at the correct door and the relevant objective is active.

05

For any other garage or robbery safe, inspect nearby clues and mission notes rather than blindly applying 0420 or 7700.

06

Some safes use codes tied to the specific job or encounter. Prefer a named, corroborated source and record the lock's location before trusting an online list.

07

Frequently asked questions

What is the GTA 6 Lab Fees lab door code?

0420 is the reported Moonstone Boutique laboratory keypad code in the Lab Fees mission.

What is Brian's lockup code in GTA 6?

7700 opens Brian's lockup in the Lab Fees storyline, as reported by GTA BOOM.

Why does 0420 not work at Brian's lockup?

Because 0420 belongs to the Moonstone Boutique lab door; Brian's lockup uses 7700.

Are all GTA 6 robbery safe codes the same?

No. Some locks have unique codes or mission-specific clues. Find the correct named location first.

Can I skip gathering the code clue?

Do not assume every mission accepts a code before its objective triggers; follow the current mission instructions.
