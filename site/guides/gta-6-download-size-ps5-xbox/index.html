GTA 6 download size & required storage on PS5 and Xbox

Understand the verified PS5 minimum and why Xbox space may vary.

CONFIRMED UPDATED NOV 18, 20265 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

PlayStation’s official GTA VI page recommends clearing a minimum of 212 GB on PS5. Do not assume the exact Xbox install size is identical; check your Xbox download queue or store listing.

GTA 6 storage requirements by platform

Make a distinction between free space recommended before installation and the final size of downloaded game files. PlayStation's GTA VI listing advises making 212 GB available. It is not a guarantee that the Xbox download or every post-launch build will have exactly the same file size.

Keep large downloads on supported playable storage. A USB drive can be useful to park games, but PS5 games cannot launch from ordinary USB extended storage. Check our free storage calculator to estimate the space you still need.

Step-by-step guide

01

On PS5 go to Settings → Storage and inspect free Console Storage or installed M.2 SSD space.

02

Keep at least 212 GB free for the PS5 game; leave extra headroom for patches and downloads.

03

On Xbox open My games & apps → Manage → Storage devices, then consult your GTA VI install detail for the current size.

04

Remove or move older games if needed; use the storage checker tool to calculate your gap.

Frequently asked questions

How many GB is GTA 6?

PlayStation recommends at least 212 GB free on PS5; actual downloaded and installed sizes can differ.

Is 212 GB the confirmed Xbox size?

No. Verify the Xbox size in its game detail/install queue.

Can I use a normal USB hard drive to play?

On PS5, PS5 titles must be played from internal or compatible M.2 SSD storage, not USB. Xbox Series X|S optimized games generally need internal or supported expansion-card storage.
