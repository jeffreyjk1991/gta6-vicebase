GTA 6 preload button missing: what to check

The usual causes: wrong edition, account, region, storage and console update.

CONFIRMED UPDATED NOV 18, 20264 MIN READ
QUICK ANSWER

Rockstar says preload is available. If the button is absent, validate your preorder, software update, account region and library before assuming an outage.

Start with the symptom, not a factory reset

What you seeFirst safe check
Preload button missingVerify edition ownership and that you are signed into the purchasing console account
Download stuck at 0%Review queue, free space and platform network/service status
Preload complete but Play is lockedCompare your console store's local unlock time with the official schedule
Install stops with a storage errorCheck supported playable storage, not only the capacity of an external USB drive

A preload is not early access: downloaded files may stay locked until the retailer's official release time. Avoid third-party "unlock patches," account-sharing offers and download links that ask for passwords. You can verify launch timing in the release time guide.

Step-by-step guide

01

Check the right account and the correct GTA VI purchased edition.

02

Confirm you are on an officially supported PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S.

03

Check the current system date, console update and network connection.

04

Look in both Library/Owned Games and the store product page for Install or Download.

05

Read the Rockstar preload FAQ and platform support before using third-party fixes.
