    QUICK ANSWERS

    Common places players get stuck

    Use these first checks even if your mission isn't in the helper yet. Advice is conditional on the objective and options visible in your game.

    I can't find the next objective

    Start with the current instruction. A missing marker and an unfinished objective need different checks.

    1. Pause and read the current objective exactly as it appears. Note whether it asks you to travel, meet someone, use an item or make contact.
    2. Return to the last instruction you completed. Look for an on-screen interaction or contact request before leaving the area.
    3. If a mission name is visible, choose it above for a targeted hint or use the linked walkthrough directory. Do not assume every available mission must be played in one fixed order.
    Browse mission walkthroughsExplore the map
    A mission won't progress

    Try these checks before replaying a whole mission. The exact on-screen instruction is more useful than guessing what should happen next.

    1. Read the current objective and compare it with what you actually did. Reaching a place may still require talking, collecting or using a marked entrance.
    2. Look around the objective area for an interaction prompt. Try the marked approach rather than forcing an unmarked shortcut.
    3. Check whether a companion or required vehicle has reached the objective with you. Wait for any active dialogue or animation to finish.
    4. Use a mission-specific hint below if your mission is covered. Otherwise open the walkthrough directory and match the exact mission title.
    5. If the game appears frozen or an interaction repeatedly fails, record the objective and game version, then use the technical Help Center. Avoid deleting saved progress.
    Mission walkthrough directoryCrashes, freezes and loading help
    I can't reach a destination

    Treat the in-game objective and navigation as the authority. Our community map is a discovery aid, not a verified mission route.

    1. Recheck the destination and route in your game. Zoom in before the last turn so you can see nearby roads, entrances or a change in elevation.
    2. If the direct approach is blocked, look for a marked entrance or a road around the site. Do not assume a visible door can be opened.
    3. Check whether the objective requires a particular vehicle, passenger or approach. Follow the current instruction before choosing a shortcut.
    Open the community mapFind comfortable camera settings
    Driving or a chase is too difficult

    Use a controlled retry to identify whether the problem is the route, visibility or steering. These are practice suggestions, not measured vehicle rankings.

    1. Read the fail message or objective first. Work out whether you need to follow, escape, reach a destination or keep something intact.
    2. On your next attempt, brake before a sharp turn, steer smoothly and build speed after the exit. Focus on completing the route before trying shortcuts.
    3. For a boat objective, check whether you need to slow down or approach a marker from another side. If you are in Gone Fishing, select that mission for a specific hint.
    Open the settings finderDriving and route guides
    Combat or escaping keeps failing

    Some objectives are about reaching safety or interacting with a target. Check the instruction before assuming every opponent must be defeated.

    1. Read the objective and last fail message. Identify whether the game asks you to fight, protect, escape or reach a marked location.
    2. Before the next attempt, check your available equipment and the controls for aiming, movement and taking cover in the current control scheme.
    3. Use the environment to reduce exposure and move with a clear destination in mind. If the objective is to escape, prioritize a safe route.
    Aim, camera and accessibility settingsFind your mission walkthrough
    I can't switch characters

    A control that works in free roam may be restricted by the current story sequence. Follow the active prompt before forcing a different action.

    1. Check whether you are in a mission, cutscene or required interaction. Finish the current instruction before trying to switch again.
    2. Read the character-switching guide and compare its conditions with your current progress. Do not assume the feature is available at every point.
    3. Check your selected control scheme in the game instead of copying a button combination for another console.
    Character-switching guideController troubleshooting
    I can't save or find my progress

    Keep your current save intact while you establish what is missing. Saving a checklist here does not save your game.

    1. Check which in-game save or autosave you loaded and compare its timestamp with your last session.
    2. If saving is unavailable during an active sequence, finish the current objective and read the save guide before exiting.
    3. Do not overwrite or delete another slot while investigating missing progress. Keep any existing local and cloud versions until you understand their timestamps.
    4. Use the Help Center for missing saves or cloud-sync problems. Follow the instructions for your console before making changes to stored data.
    How saving worksMissing-save troubleshooting
    The game is frozen, crashing or loading

    A frozen screen or crash needs a different checklist from a mission puzzle. Start with the console-specific Help Center.

    1. Note your console, the exact error text and whether the failure occurs at the same objective each time.
    2. Open the Help Center below and choose crashing, loading or the matching symptom.
    3. Check game and console updates. Preserve save data while following the suggested first checks.
    4. If the issue repeats, keep the mission name and game version ready for Rockstar Support.

    Mission help: common questions

    What should I do if I am stuck in a GTA 6 mission?

    Read the current objective, identify the missing interaction, and choose a problem in the helper. For a covered early mission, reveal a hint first and open the solution only if you need it. A frozen game belongs in the technical Help Center.

    How do I find my next GTA 6 mission?

    Check your active objective, map legend and any contact instruction. Review the in-game UI navigation and prompt settings if markers are difficult to see. Match your mission name with a walkthrough instead of assuming there is one fixed mission order.

    Does this cover every GTA 6 mission?

    No. Detailed hints currently cover Prologue, Gone Fishing, Gear Up and Lab Fees, attributed to VGC's published walkthroughs. General checklists help with objectives, routes, driving, combat, character switching and saves. The linked walkthrough directory offers broader coverage.

    Will the mission helper reveal story spoilers?

    The initial checklist avoids story outcomes. Mission hints appear one at a time when you ask. Exact solutions and codes require a separate reveal action. Linked external walkthroughs may contain spoilers.

    Can I save my checklist and return later?

    Yes. Select Save plan to keep the console, problem, mission choice and completed checklist steps in this browser. Your objective note is not saved. Use Resume saved plan on your next visit, or Clear saved plan to remove it. This does not connect to your game save.

    What happens to the objective text I enter?

    It is used only on this page to suggest a matching problem or covered mission. It is not sent to GTASIX, saved by this helper, copied into the checklist or included in shared links.
