GTA 6 questions answered

When is GTA 6 released?

Rockstar lists November 19, 2026 for the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S release.

Will GTA 6 launch on PC?

A PC date and official PC specifications have not been announced in Rockstar’s published launch information.

Who are the two central GTA 6 characters?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.

Is the full GTA 6 map confirmed?

No. Rockstar names multiple Leonida regions, but pre-release building-level maps are community reconstructions.

What storage space is needed on PS5?

PlayStation recommends freeing at least 212 GB on PS5. Actual file download size is not the same as the advised space target.
