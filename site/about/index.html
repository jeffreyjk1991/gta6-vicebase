What is ViceBase?

ViceBase is a free, independent resource library dedicated to Grand Theft Auto VI. Its goal is simple: when a player searches for a GTA 6 question, a useful answer should be easy to find, easy to follow, and supported by evidence.

What you will find

Launch and preload help, storage expansion, download errors, hardware explainers, official footage, settings, gameplay primers and, after release and verification, missions, vehicles, map locations and collectibles. The first edition has 36 individual guides across eight topic categories, plus a detailed, community-reconstructed Leonida map and eight official character profiles.
