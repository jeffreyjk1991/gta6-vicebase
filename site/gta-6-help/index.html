How to report a persistent GTA 6 problem

Note your console, exact error code, game version, whether it crashes in a particular mission, and which safe steps you already tried. Attach screenshots or a video if support asks. Use Rockstar Support, PlayStation's game-launch guide or Xbox's error-code search.

Important: If your console overheats, turns off unexpectedly, or displays a hardware safety warning, stop repeated attempts and follow the manufacturer's hardware support. We do not recommend factory reset, save deletion or third-party repair utilities.

Frequently asked questions

Why does GTA 6 keep crashing on PS5 or Xbox?

Players have reported crashes, including during the prologue. There is no single proven fix. Start with game and console updates, restart fully, record exact error codes and consult Rockstar and console support.

What do I do if GTA 6 is stuck on a white or black loading screen?

Close and relaunch once, update the game and console, check official console support and avoid repeatedly deleting save data. If the issue is reproducible at one scene, report it with version and timestamp.

How can I fix GTA 6 downloading slowly?

Check PS5 or Xbox download queue, available storage, network status and the platform's own troubleshooting guidance. Do not delete your game files as the first step.

Why is GTA 6 locked on PS5?

A padlock may indicate an account-entitlement or license problem. Confirm the purchasing account, check platform service status and use PlayStation's Restore Licenses steps.

How do I recover a missing GTA 6 save?

Do not overwrite or delete local/cloud saves while investigating. Check the in-game manual save slots, then the platform's cloud-save management before taking destructive action.

Is GTA 6 blurry or running at low FPS?

Rockstar has described a 30 FPS console target. Test a lower motion-blur setting, HDR calibration and TV Game Mode. A blurry image alone does not prove a defect or a hidden 60 FPS mode.

Is there a confirmed solution to every GTA 6 bug?

No. Community issues are reports, not confirmed diagnoses. This guide provides reversible checks and official support routes, not guaranteed cures.
