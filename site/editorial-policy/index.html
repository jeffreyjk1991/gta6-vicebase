Editorial standards

Verified first, speculation clearly marked.

How we check information

Our first choice is Rockstar Games and Rockstar Support, followed by official PlayStation and Xbox documentation. We link sources inside each guide so you can check the same information directly.

What CONFIRMED means

A claim is supported by an official source as of the visible review date. That does not mean game-mechanics instructions have been independently tested in a pre-release build. "VERIFY AT LAUNCH" means the guide describes safe preparation, a verification framework or a future workflow, not claimed discoveries.

How we handle rumors

We do not invent cheat codes, locations, missions, PC requirements, FPS targets, collectible coordinates or gameplay footage for search traffic. Confirmed and unconfirmed material stays clearly separated. We favor helpful pages over hundreds of nearly identical keyword pages.

Corrections and updates

Dates, store policies and hardware guidance can change. Consult the linked official sources for the latest. Each page shows its own review date. Mission hints name the published walkthrough they summarize; editorial checklists are practical suggestions, not claims of independent gameplay testing.
